What ClipEngine is

ClipEngine is a personal automation tool operated by a single developer. It assembles short highlight videos from creators who allow their content to be clipped, credits every creator in the video description, and posts the result to the developer's own social media accounts (YouTube and, where enabled, TikTok). It is not offered to the public: there are no sign-ups, user accounts, or paid services.

Who may use it

Only the developer and people the developer has explicitly authorized may run ClipEngine, and only to post to accounts they own or are authorized to manage. By connecting an account to ClipEngine, you confirm you have that authority.

Content and creators' rights

All clips belong to their original creators. ClipEngine is used only with creators who permit clipping of their content, and every video credits and links the creators it features. Creators who want their content removed, or who no longer want to be clipped, can ask using the contact link below; their videos will be taken down and they will be removed from the list of clipped creators.

Platform rules

Everything ClipEngine posts must follow the rules of the platform it is posted to, including the YouTube Terms of Service, TikTok Terms of Service and community guidelines, and the Twitch Terms of Service for clip sources. ClipEngine does not try to get around platform limits, and videos are posted privately or as drafts for a person to review before they are made public.
