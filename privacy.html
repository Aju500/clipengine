Privacy Policy

ClipEngine. Last updated October 21, 2026.

What ClipEngine is

ClipEngine is a personal automation tool operated by a single developer to upload short gaming compilations to the developer's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data we access

ClipEngine requests two Google OAuth scopes for the developer's own Google account only:

  • youtube.upload: to upload finished videos to the developer's channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read public video listings from whitelisted creators' channels in order to find clip sources.

How data is used and stored

The OAuth token is stored only on the developer's own computer and is used only to perform the actions above. ClipEngine does not collect, sell, share, or transfer Google user data to any third party, and does not use it for advertising. No data from any other user is accessed.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. ClipEngine's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact
